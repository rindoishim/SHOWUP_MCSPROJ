## Platform

web

## Users
- Homeowners and small contractors in Bagumbong, Caloocan City and nearby areas. They buy construction, electrical and plumbing materials for a specific job and often don't know the exact quantities they need.
- Vreymar staff: an admin, cashiers on the counter POS and inventory staff. They use the staff portal all day on a desktop at the store.

## Product Purpose
An online storefront and staff system for Vreymar Hardware. Customers browse the catalog, estimate materials for a project, order for delivery or pickup, and file replacement requests. Staff run the POS, inventory, orders and deliveries, reports and users. Success means customers buy the right quantities the first time and staff stop tracking stock and deliveries on paper.

## Positioning
Estimate AI turns a project description, a blueprint and site details into an itemised material list priced from Vreymar's own shelves. Customers then choose a delivery date and a two-hour window. A generic online hardware catalog can't truthfully claim either.

## Operating Context
- Counter sales on the POS, cash, GCash and Maya payments, scheduled truck deliveries, store pickup.
- Replacement flow per the Final Defense paper, Fig 7.5: review, approve or reject, stock check, then waiting for stock or refund.
- 12% VAT. Prices in PHP (₱).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS prototype with localStorage as the data layer. The paper plans Laravel/Supabase later.
- Estimate AI is currently rule-based. It requires a blueprint upload and site details.
- Product photos: none yet. Image slots use placeholders until the owner supplies photos (confirmed by the user 2026-11-18).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Vreymar Hardware, Bagumbong, Caloocan.
- Inferred, not confirmed: no fixed logo or brand colours. The palette may be chosen freely.
- Voice: plain, practical, sentence case, Filipino context (peso, barangay, GCash).

## Evidence on Hand
- One hero photograph: assets/img/hero.jpg (construction worker).
- No testimonials, reviews, press or customer counts. Never fabricate them.

## Product Principles
1. The right quantity, the first time: estimates and stock are always tied to the live catalog.
2. Clear terms: delivery, payment, replacements and refunds are stated plainly before checkout.
3. Counter speed: staff screens favour fast scanning and large hit targets over decoration.
