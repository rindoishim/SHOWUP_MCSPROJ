Thirty years at the same counter.

Vreymar Hardware is a family-run store owned by Vince Reymar Zuñiga. For more than three decades it has supplied homeowners, DIY builders and contractors in Bagumbong with cement, steel, plumbing and electrical materials.

About forty customers come to the counter every day. For years every estimate was worked out by hand and every sale written in a logbook. This site moves that work online: live stock, scheduled delivery, and Estimate AI, which lists the materials a project needs and what they cost before you buy.

Terms & Conditions

What you agree to when you create an account or place an order online. Questions? Call the store before ordering.

Draft for the store owner's review · prototype

  1. Your account

    Keep your password private; you are responsible for orders placed from your account. Staff accounts are created only by the store admin. We may deactivate accounts used for fraudulent orders.

  2. Orders and confirmation

    An order is a request until the store confirms it. We check stock and, for GCash or Maya orders, your payment screenshot before confirming. You may cancel online while an order is still pending; after confirmation, call the store.

  3. Prices, VAT and fees

    Prices are in Philippine pesos and may change without notice; the price shown when you place the order is the price you pay. 12% VAT is added at checkout. Delivery is ₱149 within Caloocan and nearby areas, free for orders of ₱5,000 and up. Store pickup is always free.

  4. If an item becomes unavailable

    Online stock is live, but counter sales happen at the same time, so an item can run out after you order it. If that happens before we prepare your order, we will contact you within one business day and you choose:

    • Wait for restock. We hold your order and give you an expected restock date. If it has not arrived within 14 days, we refund that item.
    • Take an equivalent item of the same or higher grade at the same price, only with your approval.
    • Refund that item. The rest of your order proceeds as scheduled.

    If we cannot reach you within two business days, we deliver the available items and refund the unavailable ones.

  5. Delivery and pickup

    You choose a date and a two-hour window; fully booked dates and slots cannot be selected. Someone aged 18 or over must receive and check the items. One reschedule is free; repeated failed deliveries may be charged the delivery fee again. Pickup orders are held for 3 days after the chosen slot, then cancelled and refunded.

  6. Bulk orders

    Orders of ₱50,000 and up must be scheduled at least 7 days ahead so we can reserve the materials. The store may ask for a down payment before reserving large quantities.

  7. Payment and refunds

    We accept cash on delivery, cash at pickup, GCash and Maya. Cashless orders need an uploaded payment screenshot. Refunds go back through the original method, GCash or Maya, or cash at the counter for cash orders, within 3–7 business days of approval.

  8. Estimate AI

    Estimates are guides based on what you enter and on standard construction ratios. They can be wrong. Check quantities with your builder or engineer before ordering; we cannot accept returns of excess materials bought from an estimate unless the item is defective.

  9. Your data

    We use your name, contact details and address only to process orders, deliveries and replacement requests, as required by the Data Privacy Act of 2012. We do not sell your information.

Return & Replacement Policy

How to report a defective, damaged, wrong or incomplete item, and what happens next.

Report a problem

  1. How a request moves

    You file itFrom your order history, within 7 days
    We check itEligibility is checked automatically
    Admin reviewsApprove or reject within 1–3 business days
    Stock checkReplacement sent, or Waiting for Stock

  2. What you can claim

    Items from a delivered or picked-up online order, reported within 7 days, that are defective, damaged in transit, the wrong item, or incomplete. Keep the item and its packaging; we may ask for photos or inspect it.

  3. What we cannot take back

    • Cut-to-length items such as wire per meter and long-span roofing, unless defective.
    • Opened or mixed cement, grout and adhesive; tinted or opened paint.
    • Items damaged by misuse, weather after delivery, or improper installation.
    • Excess materials bought from an estimate, unless defective.

  4. Quotation

    When you file, you see the refund value of the claimed units (item price with VAT). The delivery fee is refunded only when the whole order was wrong or damaged.

  5. When the replacement is out of stock

    An approved replacement is checked against stock. If we don't have it, the request becomes Waiting for Stock. You decide:

    • Wait. We send the replacement free of charge as soon as it arrives.
    • Take a refund at any time while waiting, with one tap in My account → Replacements.

    You can also choose "refund me instead" when filing. If stock hasn't arrived after 14 days, we refund you automatically.

  6. Rejected requests

    If a request does not meet this policy, we tell you why in your account. You can call the store to discuss it.
